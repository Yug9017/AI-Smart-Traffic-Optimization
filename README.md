# Smart-Traffic-Management-System
This is the Traffic Management System, a robust solution designed to optimize urban traffic flow, reduce congestion, and enhance road safety. It leverages advanced technologies to provide real-time monitoring, intelligent control, and actionable insights for transportation authorities and urban planners.

# Features 
1. Real-time Traffic Monitoring – Collects and processes live data from various sources (sensors, cameras) to provide an accurate view of road conditions.

2. Dynamic Traffic Light Control – Intelligently adjusts traffic light timings based on current traffic density, emergency vehicle priority, and predictive analytics to minimize waiting times.

3. Route Optimization & Guidance – Offers optimized routing suggestions to drivers, helping them avoid congested areas and reach destinations faster.

4. Incident Detection & Response – Automatically detects accidents, breakdowns, and other road incidents, alerting authorities and facilitating rapid response.

5. Data Visualization & Analytics – Provides comprehensive dashboards and reports for historical data analysis, trend identification, and performance evaluation.

6. Scalable Architecture – Designed to seamlessly integrate new sensors, intersections, and data sources as urban infrastructure expands.

7. Intuitive Admin Dashboard – A user-friendly interface for managing system parameters, viewing alerts, and monitoring overall system health.
